# Big Blue Desk

A working tool for Big Blue Vanuatu. Drop in the exports Big Blue already has (Bookeo bookings, Xero invoices, PayZen payments, optionally WooCommerce orders). In a few seconds it:

- matches every booking to its Xero invoice and its PayZen payments, and lists exactly what needs doing
- builds the dive-day manifest for any date, with the problems to sort before the boat leaves, plus a message for the crew
- writes payment reminders in English or French
- shows invoiced revenue by Xero account (4-1020 Dive Courses, 4-1040 Rentals Sales, and the rest by code)
- exports the fix list (Excel or CSV), the day list and the reminders

Everything runs in the browser. Files are read on the computer they are dropped on and never uploaded, and nothing in Bookeo, Xero or PayZen is changed.

## Try it

Open `index.html` (double-click works) and press **Try it with sample files**, or drop in real exports. Excel (.xlsx) and CSV with any separator both work. If a column is not recognised, the box says so and every column can be re-pointed under "Check or change".

The sample files in `samples/` are invented (customers, emails and amounts). They cover every case the desk looks for.

## What it checks

| Finding | How it is found | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Unpaid, diving this week | Nothing received, dive within 7 days of the check date | Remind today or take payment on arrival |
| Paid, but still open in Xero | PayZen payment or Bookeo "paid", but the Xero invoice still has money due | Mark the invoice paid in Xero |
| Not in Xero | No invoice quotes the booking number, and none matches on email or name with the same amount within 45 days | Create the invoice |
| Paid twice | More than one successful payment and more money in than the booking is worth (a 50/50 deposit is not flagged) | Refund the extra |
| Payment failed or link expired | Only refused, expired or abandoned PayZen attempts | Send a new link |
| Amounts differ | Bookeo total and Xero total differ by more than 1% | Correct one of them |
| Cancelled, invoice still open | Booking cancelled, Xero invoice open or draft | Void or credit note |
| Paid in Xero, unpaid in Bookeo | Xero paid, Bookeo shows nothing paid | Mark it paid in Bookeo |
| Payment with no booking | A successful payment nothing can be matched to (shop orders are recognised when the WooCommerce export is added) | Find the booking |
| Overdue / Awaiting payment | Unpaid, invoice past its due date / not yet due | Reminder / nothing yet |

Matching order: the booking number on the invoice (number, reference or line description), then email and amount, then name and amount; payments by order reference, then email and amount. Digit runs shorter than four, and years, never link two records.

## Files

```
index.html             the page (loads the three scripts below with plain script tags)
src/tablekit.js        reads CSV and .xlsx, finds columns by name, reads money and dates, writes .xlsx and .csv
src/diveDesk.js        the checks, the manifest, reminders and revenue
src/samples.js         the sample files for the "Try it" button (generated by build.py from samples/)
samples/*.csv          invented sample exports
tests/verify.js        node tests on the reader and the desk (same files as the page)
tests/browser-check.py drives the real page in Chromium
tests/make-fixture.py  writes tests/fixtures/bookeo-SAMPLE.xlsx, a real Excel file for the reader tests
```

The tests load the same `src/` files the page uses, so what is tested is what runs.

## What the connected version adds

This page works on exports. Connected to Big Blue's own Bookeo, Xero and PayZen accounts, the same checks run by themselves every morning: paid bookings are marked paid in Xero, missing invoices are created, the manifest goes to the crew the evening before, and only what needs a person is sent on. That part uses Big Blue's own accounts and keys, set up with Big Blue.
